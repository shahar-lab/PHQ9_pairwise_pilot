How data/collected/*.csv becomes data/processed/likert_processed.csv and pairwise_processed.csv, stage by stage, with the actual counts from the current data/raw run.

Collect converting_to_data_raw.R

Every Pavlovia/jsPsych session CSV in data/collected/ is read and row-bound into one long table, tagged with its source file.

in: 10 session CSVs out: data_raw.csv
↓ 10 participants carried forward
Build item lookup build_item_lookup.R

The 16 PHQ-9 item codes (1, 2a, 2b, … 8b, attn_check) seen in the likert phase are recoded to a clean integer item id, in a fixed clinical order.

in: data_raw.csv out: item_lookup.csv · 16 items
↓
Reshape into task tables converting_likert_pairwise.R

data_raw.csv is split by phase and joined against the item lookup: one row per rating for the likert phase, one row per trial (with both items, choice and RT) for the pairwise phase.

out: likert.csv · 160 rows out: pairwise.csv · 1050 rows
↓ still all 10 participants
QC — raw qc_report_raw.R

Per-participant trial counts, skip counts, window-abort counts and RT summaries are tabulated before any exclusion, as a baseline reference.

out: qc_likert_raw.pdf out: qc_pairwise_raw.pdf
↓
Filter & exclude filter_bad_trials.R

One participant-level check decides who stays. Skip count plays no role here — it neither excludes a participant nor causes any trial row to be dropped; skipped pairwise trials are kept in the processed data like any other trial.
